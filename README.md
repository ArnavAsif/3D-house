# Villa Lumina: Premium Single-Story Modern Luxury Home & 3D Interactive Product Showroom

A state-of-the-art interactive WebGL / Three.js 3D luxury residence and virtual product showroom, designed in accordance with modern luxury architectural standards and the design specifications from the project brief.

## Key Features

1. **Realistic Luxury Architecture**:
   - **Footprint**: $20.0\text{ m} \times 14.5\text{ m}$ ($194.2\text{ m}^2$ enclosed living area).
   - **Exterior Walls**: $0.30\text{ m}$ buildable thickness with floor-to-ceiling glass fenestrations.
   - **Interior Partition Walls**: $0.15\text{ m}$ acoustic thickness with clear passage lintels.
   - **Finished Ceiling Height**: $3.20\text{ m}$ with recessed warm LED architectural light coves.
   - **Doors**: Monumental $1.6\text{ m} \times 2.8\text{ m}$ architectural timber pivot front door with $1.8\text{ m}$ brass pull handle; flush interior doors; wide garden sliding doors.
   - **Flooring & Materials**: Honed Calacatta marble slab, Roman beige travertine, Scandinavian white oak parquet, and black anodized aluminum window mullions.

2. **Required Room Zones**:
   - **Main Entrance & Porch**: Raised stone plinth, steps, planters with lush monstera/ficus, exterior sconces.
   - **Wide Entrance Hallway / Foyer**: $3.5\text{ m}$ wide reception spine with unobstructed sightlines.
   - **Open-Plan Living Room**: Sunken-feel lounge with modular cream sectional sofa, dual travertine tables, fluted oak TV media wall, and arc floor lamp.
   - **Formal Dining Area**: Solid American walnut 8-seater table, upholstered armchairs, and suspended architectural linear pendant.
   - **Modern Chef Kitchen**: Monolithic $3.2\text{ m}$ Calacatta marble waterfall island, 4 leather barstools, undermount black sink, and floor-to-ceiling integrated cabinetry.
   - **Secondary Showroom / Master Suite**: Dedicated product gallery featuring a floating oak platform bed, acoustic slat headboard, organic boucle armchair, and private garden patio access.
   - **Spa Bathroom**: Floating carved travertine double vanity, backlit mirror, fluted glass walk-in shower, wall-hung toilet.
   - **Rear Landscaped Garden**: Stone patio pavers, green lawn, bamboo cluster wall, and landscape uplighting.

3. **Fluid First-Person Walking Circulation**:
   - **Continuous Walking Loop**: Entrance $\rightarrow$ Foyer $\rightarrow$ Living Room $\rightarrow$ Showroom Suite $\rightarrow$ Garden $\rightarrow$ Kitchen $\rightarrow$ Dining $\rightarrow$ Foyer.
   - Minimum walking clearance of $1.5\text{ m}$ throughout all areas (zero dead ends or awkward choke points).
   - Built-in collision detection against walls, island, table, bed, and sofa.

4. **Multi-Camera Modes**:
   - **Dollhouse 3D Floor Plan**: Axonometric 3D perspective cutaway view (matching the top overview in the reference image) with orbit controls.
   - **First-Person Walkthrough (FPS)**: $1.65\text{ m}$ eye level, WASD / Arrow keys, mouse drag look, and mobile virtual touch joystick.
   - **Top-Down Blueprint**: Planimetric architectural layout with north alignment.
   - **Room Fast Teleport**: One-click quick jump pills for instant tour navigation.

5. **Interactive Product Showroom & Commerce Engine**:
   - 14 interactive products with 3D floating beacon pins and hover glow effects.
   - Product detail modal matching the reference card design: specifications, dimensions, materials, and live color variant switcher (updates 3D model color in real-time).
   - Shopping cart drawer with quantity stepper, subtotal, and checkout flow.
   - Real-time 2D minimap radar with live player position marker and viewing cone.
   - Day / Night lighting toggle (Natural sunlight vs Warm 2700K evening architectural glow).
